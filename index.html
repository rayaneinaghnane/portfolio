About Me

Based in Rabat, Morocco, I am a filmmaker and multimedia engineer passionate about telling authentic human stories through the lens of contemporary documentary cinema.

I hold a Master's degree in Multimedia Engineering and Digital Creativity from Ibn Tofail University. I work across filmmaking, photography, design, and creative production to create immersive, emotionally resonant experiences.

Open to opportunities in Rabat and other cities, I am seeking roles that align with my passion for visual storytelling, creative direction, and multimedia production.

My practice is rooted in artistic research and a deep fascination with memory, identity, and social narratives. Visual storytelling has the power to illuminate the human condition and foster meaningful connections between people and ideas.

Whether directing documentary projects, creating visual art, designing interactive experiences, or managing creative teams, I bring intentionality, curiosity, and craftsmanship to every project.
